Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 26, 2026

Our Commitment: Digital Sponsor is designed with privacy at its core. We believe your recovery journey is personal and should remain private.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Email address and name when you register
  • Step Work Data: Your responses to step work exercises (stored securely and never shared)
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics to improve our service
  • Device Information: Browser type and device type for compatibility

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize our recovery support services
  • To save your progress in step work exercises
  • To send service-related communications (if you opt in)
  • To improve our platform based on anonymous usage patterns

3. Data Protection

Your privacy is paramount:

  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Step work responses are private and never shared with third parties
  • We use secure Azure cloud infrastructure
  • You can delete all your data at any time

4. Third-Party Services

We use limited third-party services:

  • Microsoft Azure: Cloud hosting and database services
  • Google AdSense: To display relevant advertisements (you can opt out via Google's ad settings)

5. Advertising

We display advertisements to support our free service:

  • We block ads for alcohol, gambling, and other potentially triggering content
  • Ads are never shown during crisis support interactions
  • Google AdSense may use cookies to show relevant ads
  • You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ad Settings

6. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Keeping you logged in
  • Remembering your preferences
  • Analytics (anonymous)
  • Advertising (via Google AdSense)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

8. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Digital Sponsor is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: privacy@digitalsponsor.ai
  • Or use our Contact Form
