## 🎯 What Is Digital Sponsor?

Digital Sponsor is an AI companion that helps AA members:
- **Work through the 12 Steps** with guided worksheets and reflection questions
- **Access AA literature instantly** - Big Book, 12&12, Daily Reflections, and more
- **Get answers to recovery questions** based on AA principles and traditions
- **Track your progress** including sobriety date, step work, and daily inventory

## ⚠️ Important: What Digital Sponsor Is NOT

Digital Sponsor is a **supplement**, not a replacement for:
- ❌ Your human sponsor's wisdom and accountability
- ❌ Attending AA meetings and fellowship
- ❌ Professional medical or mental health treatment
- ❌ Crisis intervention (call 911 or AA Hotline for emergencies)

**Nothing replaces human connection in recovery.** Use Digital Sponsor as an additional tool in your toolkit.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. All your step work and personal data is encrypted end-to-end. We cannot read your inventory or personal reflections. You can export or delete your data at any time.

**Q: Does this replace my sponsor?**
A: Absolutely not. Digital Sponsor is a tool to help you between meetings and sponsor calls. It cannot replace the human relationship, accountability, and experience of working with a sponsor.

**Q: Is this affiliated with AA World Services?**
A: No. Digital Sponsor is an independent project created by AA members for AA members. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Alcoholics Anonymous World Services, Inc.

**Q: What if the AI gives wrong information?**
A: Always verify important information with your sponsor, the actual literature, or at meetings. AI can make mistakes. When in doubt, check the source.

**Q: How do I report bugs or give feedback?**
A: Reply to this email or use the feedback form in the app. As a beta tester, your input is invaluable!

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Not yet, but the website works great on mobile browsers. Add it to your home screen for app-like access.
