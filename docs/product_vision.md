# Why Digital Sponsor 
The program Alcoholics Anonymous can be found the same titled Book called Alcoholics Anonymous.  The book was created so that an person, absent proximity to other recovered alcoholics could in theory learn an engage with the program from the book, taking action to implement the 12 steps in their own life and be sober; happy, joyous and free. There is a bit of an interesting conundrum built into this idea that makes it imperfect though - The book itself identifies AA as a social program of "one alcholic helping another" and though this is ideal, the book was made to be there for that ideal and even more importantly, for the less than idea scenario where another alcoholic or group of them is not able to be present. We took to calling the groups Meetings or groups and the one on one helping Sponsorship. Today there are meetings everywhere, almost any hour of any day you can find a meeting, and there you can find a like-minded, compatible individual to do one on one work with you, to be your "Sponsor".  So, why build a "digital sponsor"? 

Here are some truths; 
1. Largely owing to the era it was written in and the "keen alcoholic mind" the "Big Book" is confusing and difficult to understand for Alcoholics and for many of us questions will linger for the entire time we are sober. This is of course because we love to overcomplicate things, split hairs and generally try to ampplify problems by pontificating on them overly much. 
2. Alcoholism is often co-morbid with other conditions more firmly planted in the realm of mental health, many of which lead us to fear judgement, be very fragile or especially when desperate enough to try to get sober. Sponsors, human sponsors often get all kinds of ideas and give instructions that are not in the the book, or other AA approved literature. This can be problematic.
3. In a sort of 2A - for fragile people, people with nuerodivergence, the humaness of sponsors, who can be judgemental or try pushing what has worked for them on their sponsees, this can cause friction.  Also for some of us, myself included, group and even individual interactions can be quite draining mentally, emptionally and physically. 
4. Finally, there those who for various health and ability reasons, cannot leave home and in some dire cases, cannot also recieve company


It is for these reasons that we build Digital Sponsor.  Alcoholics Anonymous is a three pronged thing that includes 1. foundationally the Program found in the book of same name. 2. The fellowship, this being the meetings and groups and finally 3. the one on one work and relationships of alcoholics working together and sharing their experience strengh and hope.  While the book and the program of action are the most vital; there should be no suggestion that they other components are not important.  Therefore Digital Sponsor is desigtned to be supplmental and provide guidance about the firt - and also supplemental to the 3rd, for when one of the needs arising from the above truths is present. Every member of AA should, to their ability seek out and engage with #2 the fellowship and do so in ways that allow emotional connection and shared sense of community as it is a vital component. 

# What is Digital Sponsor
Digital Sponsor is first and foremost an AI Chat application, focused on AA approved literature that maintains some context about the user in order to better respond to any queries they have about the program on AA, the history of AA and documented experience strength and hope of others. In addition, Digitial Sponsor should provide program "work" guides that go with each step, each tradition and each principle, the should be donwloadable documents in formats commpatible with word or excel. Also, as a matter of constraint, the Digital Sponsor should take the traditions quite seriously and in particular treat Tradition 6, 7, 11 and 12 as inviolable. Meaning that queries beyond the scope of AA should be referred elsewhere and stick mainly to problems of recovery from addiction/alcholism. Digital Sponsor should ignore queries that directlly endorse outside causes (politics, any particular race, religiion or gender over any others) and not endorse any particular institution or group in keeping with 7th and 11th tradition.  Finally, tradition 12 must be of the utmost immportance. We must keep as little data as needed for proper user experiene and functioning on our users and they must be encrypted at rest and in transport. Users must be able to delete their information at any time in its entirety and should a user need to provide back their 4th step work sheet or 8th step list - it should be used only for the context in the current session and then deleted from logs and memory when the session is completed. Digital Sponsor should have a gRAG that has prefechted AA authorized/endorsed materials and have the ability to reach out for more real time information including meeting schedules local to the uesrs current location or a location they have indicated. 

We need Digital Sponsor to be ad supported if possible, darwing at least enough revenue to cover the cost. We will need both technical and business plan for making that possible. As well as alternatives that might do the same without being overly burdonsome to the general user audience. Digital Sponsor come as both website and hybrid mobile app (mostly served as mobile site, but available in app stores and accessed as an app on phone. - is it possible to keep context on the users device for web? For mobile? and read from that when it exists? Is this a good pattern or no?

## Thoughts, Questions, ASR/NFC

At least at the outset, this needs to be as cheap as possible will still giving full functionality
- Should we use local llm on cloud compute? or use Copilot Studio or Similar? 
- Probably a relatively few users at first - will need a feedback mechanism from them and automated issue alerting
- Can users provide RLHF for responses to hep hone training? Is that even a good practice? 

Need users to be able to self register and create a profile and probably setup some type of MFA, again for now lowest effectie price point
Should we let users get a few free queries in before forcing registration? 

How can we limit and filter the data sources and query responses to within the traditions and from AA approved sources? (not totally necessary for meeting schedules)
How can we put both safety and security first?

We need to make clear the mission is not to eliminate or replace fellowship or face to face, "one alcoholic working with another" but rather to serve those who for any reason are unable or would prefer to the objective and opinion free response of the Digital Sponsor. 

How can we make sure we don't run afoul of copyright and licensing issues? Since we are not trying to make a huge profit, I am not overly worrried, but would like to know risks and how to mitigate. 

The Chatbot giving clear, correct responses sourced from the graph RAG (or whatever architecture is deemed best, this is currently a guess on my part) built from AA approved literature and other AA approved literature online is the most imperative functionality. 
If queries wander into areas of mental illness/health then the user should be directed to seek the adivce of a professional psychcologist or psychiatrist. Where they get too far into religion or philosophy, refer them to proper sources. Digital Sponsor may give some indication of how these might relate to AA. Where there is an overlap with any of these things, provide the AA answer, but refer and defer to the expterise of the professionals/authorized persons in those fields. 
I have found that explainations and understanding can be helpful, but it is best to treat the program of AA like a recipe book, and at least when first doing the steps, to not get fancy and simply do exactly what is suggested in the book.
